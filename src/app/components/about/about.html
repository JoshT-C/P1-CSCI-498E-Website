About

I'm a computer science student at the Colorado School of Mines who likes the parts of a system you can open the case on.

I've run a home lab since high school — virtual machines, containers, networking, the unglamorous layer. At Mines I'm president of Minecraft at Mines, a 600+ member community: the server infrastructure behind it is something I designed, built, and operate, including an on-demand server platform the members use every day.

Summers 2025 and 2026 were at Parsons: a microservice platform in Angular, Spring Boot, and PostgreSQL — a Kubernetes migration that took it from 10 concurrent users to 1,000, and a Keycloak-backed zero-trust edge. In spring 2026 I spent six weeks at Kinective building a conversational agent on AWS Bedrock — Claude, MCP tools, and a model-cost cut from $12–15 down to under $3.

Local-first is a position, not a preference: if the machine fails, the data shouldn't die with a subscription. The stack above is the argument. This site is Angular and three.js, self-hosted on my own hardware behind nginx with Let's Encrypt — if something here is broken, the source is public and the issue tracker is open.
